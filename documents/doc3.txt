GLOBEX CORPORATION — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK 2025
============================================
Human Resources Department | Confidential Internal Document

SECTION A: COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
--------------------------------------
A.1 Salary Structure
Globex Corporation follows a banded salary structure with six compensation bands
(Band 1 through Band 6). Band 1 covers entry-level positions (annual salary range:
$45,000–$65,000). Band 6 covers senior executive roles (annual salary range:
$250,000–$500,000 plus equity). All salaries are reviewed annually during the
performance review cycle in Q4.

A.2 Health Insurance
Full-time employees are eligible for health insurance coverage starting on the
first day of the month following their 30-day probationary period. Globex offers
three plan tiers: Basic (HMO), Standard (PPO), and Premium (PPO+Dental+Vision).
The company covers 80% of the premium for the employee; dependents may be added
at the employee's expense.

A.3 Paid Time Off (PTO)
Employees accrue PTO based on their tenure:
  - 0–1 years:  15 days per year
  - 1–3 years:  18 days per year
  - 3–7 years:  22 days per year
  - 7+ years:   28 days per year
PTO accrues monthly (1/12 of the annual allocation per month). Unused PTO may be
carried over up to a maximum of 10 days into the next calendar year. PTO is paid
out upon termination at the current base rate.

A.4 Retirement Plan (401k)
Globex offers a 401(k) plan with a company match of up to 4% of the employee's
annual salary. Employees are eligible to enroll after 90 days of employment.
The company match vests fully after 3 years of continuous employment (cliff vesting).

SECTION B: WORKPLACE POLICIES
-------------------------------
B.1 Remote Work Policy
Globex operates a hybrid work model. Employees are expected to be in the office
at least three days per week (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday are the designated
anchor days). Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the department
head and HR director and are reviewed quarterly.

B.2 Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to maintain professional conduct, treat colleagues with
respect, and avoid conflicts of interest. Discrimination, harassment, and retaliation
of any kind are strictly prohibited and will result in immediate disciplinary action
up to and including termination.

B.3 Data Security and Confidentiality
Employees must not share proprietary information, client data, or trade secrets
with unauthorized parties. All company laptops are encrypted with AES-256.
Employees must use VPN when accessing internal systems remotely. Violations of
data security policies are grounds for immediate termination and may result in
legal action.

SECTION C: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
------------------------------------
C.1 Annual Review Cycle
Performance reviews are conducted annually in December. Reviews use a five-point
scale: Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Partially Meets Expectations,
Does Not Meet Expectations, and New Employee (for those with less than 6 months tenure).

C.2 Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)
Employees rated "Does Not Meet Expectations" for two consecutive review cycles will
be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan. PIPs last 90 days and include
bi-weekly check-ins with the manager and HR. Successful completion of a PIP removes
the employee from probationary status.

SECTION D: OFFBOARDING
------------------------
D.1 Resignation
Employees are requested to provide at least two weeks' written notice of resignation.
Senior roles (Band 4 and above) are asked to provide four weeks' notice. Final
paychecks are issued on the next regular pay date following the last day of work.

D.2 Involuntary Termination
In cases of involuntary termination without cause, Globex provides severance pay
equal to two weeks of base salary for every completed year of service, up to a
maximum of 26 weeks. Severance is conditional on signing a separation agreement.
