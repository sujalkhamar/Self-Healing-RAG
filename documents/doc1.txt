E-COMMERCE REFUND AND RETURNS POLICY
=====================================
Last Updated: January 2025

SECTION 1: OVERVIEW
--------------------
Our company is committed to ensuring complete customer satisfaction. This document
outlines our comprehensive refund, return, and exchange policy for all product
categories sold through our platform.

SECTION 2: PHYSICAL PRODUCTS
------------------------------
Physical products may be returned within 30 days of the original purchase date,
provided they are in their original, unused condition with all original packaging
and tags intact. Customers must obtain a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA)
number before shipping items back. Return shipping costs are the responsibility
of the customer unless the return is due to our error or a defective product.

Refunds for physical products are processed within 5-7 business days of receiving
the returned item and passing quality inspection. Refunds are issued to the original
payment method only. We do not offer cash refunds for credit card purchases.

SECTION 3: DIGITAL PRODUCTS AND DOWNLOADS
-------------------------------------------
Digital products, including software licenses, e-books, downloadable templates,
online courses, and digital subscriptions, are governed by a strict no-refund
policy once the product has been accessed, downloaded, or the license key has
been revealed. This policy exists because digital products cannot be "returned"
in the traditional sense once delivered.

Exceptions to the digital product no-refund rule:
  a) Technical failure: If the digital product is demonstrably non-functional
     due to a defect on our part, and we are unable to provide a working
     replacement within 72 hours, a full refund will be issued.
  b) Unauthorized purchase: If you believe a purchase was made without your
     authorization, contact our fraud department within 48 hours.
  c) Pre-purchase cancellation: If you cancel a digital product order before
     it is delivered or the download link is generated, a full refund applies.
  d) Subscription services: Monthly subscriptions may be cancelled at any time;
     however, the current billing period will not be refunded. Annual
     subscriptions may be refunded on a pro-rated basis within the first 14
     days of the subscription term.

SECTION 4: BUNDLES AND COMBO PRODUCTS
---------------------------------------
If a bundle contains both physical and digital components, the physical component
follows the 30-day return policy. The digital component follows the digital
no-refund policy unless an exception listed in Section 3 applies.

SECTION 5: HOW TO REQUEST A REFUND
-------------------------------------
To initiate a refund request:
  1. Log into your account at our website.
  2. Navigate to "Order History" and select the relevant order.
  3. Click "Request Refund" and complete the refund request form.
  4. Our support team will respond within 2 business days.
  5. Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

For urgent cases, contact support@example-store.com or call 1-800-555-0199.

SECTION 6: CHARGEBACKS AND DISPUTES
--------------------------------------
We strongly encourage customers to contact us before initiating a chargeback
with their bank or credit card provider. Unauthorized chargebacks may result in
account suspension. We work diligently to resolve disputes fairly and promptly.

SECTION 7: PROMOTIONAL AND DISCOUNTED ITEMS
---------------------------------------------
Items purchased at a discount of 50% or more during promotional sales are
considered final sale and are not eligible for returns or refunds, except in
cases of product defect or fulfillment error.
